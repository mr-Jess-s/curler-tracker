# Curler Tracker community contribution process

## Goal
Let the curling community help build and correct the knowledge base without allowing unverified submissions to become asserted facts.

## Appropriate submissions
- current or former competition names / aliases;
- roster, team, club, coach, event, result, or qualification corrections;
- links to official records;
- scans/photos of club records or tournament material;
- missing historical appearances;
- identity merges or splits that need review.

## Submission states
Inbox -> Triaged -> Evidence review -> Confirmed / Unresolved / Rejected -> Applied -> Verified

## Evidence rule
A submission identifies something to investigate. The underlying curling fact still requires evidence.

## Name-change rule
Never infer why a curler changed their name. Record only the competition names and continuity that the evidence supports.

## Community workflow
1. Preserve the raw submission.
2. Keep any attached/source evidence.
3. Check stable athlete IDs first.
4. Compare event, team, position, club and season context.
5. Resolve conflicts explicitly.
6. Apply only supported facts.
7. Keep old and new names searchable when continuity is verified.
8. Retain provenance and reviewer notes.

## Privacy
Do not solicit or store unnecessary private family, relationship, contact, medical, financial, or other sensitive information. Public competition identity is enough.

## Future trusted contributors
Verified-contributor status may later improve routing and review speed for clubs, associations, or curlers. It must not remove provenance or verification requirements.
