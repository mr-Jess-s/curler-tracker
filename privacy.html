Privacy, coverage & corrections

Updated October 24, 2026.

What the records mean

Curler Tracker brings published competition, event, club and international championship records into one searchable view. Records are shown as curling facts without assigning an athlete to the association or place that supplied an entry, and without outbound record links. Coverage is incomplete across seasons, clubs and countries. An unsuccessful search does not prove that someone has no curling history.

The dated Canadian and World Curling member-site inventories describe source-site discovery, not competition-result coverage. Some sites were blocked, unavailable, or not listed. The live coverage note reports the inventory counts and dates. Missing history remains unknown.

A shared name alone does not establish identity. The app uses record identifiers and retained collection metadata to check identity continuity, while keeping that metadata out of the record display. An archived entry does not establish a current team, live score or complete career.

What your browser stores

The application saves the last searched name, its result snapshot, saved team names and roster snapshots, and feedback drafts in this browser. Its service worker caches application files and visited public archive data for offline use. Clear this site's browser storage to remove these items. Shared links include the searched name in the URL.

Network requests and hosting

Vercel hosts the site and may process request metadata such as IP address, user agent and requested URL for delivery and security. Searches use provenance-preserved Curling I/O snapshots collected by Arthur and served from this site's CDN. Missing snapshots remain explicitly unavailable rather than triggering visitor-side source traffic or silently loading another branch's records. Vercel receives ordinary connection metadata and applies its own policies. This build has no advertising trackers or application analytics SDK.

Feedback and corrections

The Feedback control prepares a report locally; copying or downloading it does not submit it. You may submit a nonsensitive correction through the project's public issue tracker. Include the source URL and the specific record needing review. GitHub requires an account and makes issues public. Do not post private information, identity documents, contact details or information about a child there.

A dedicated private privacy-request channel is not yet available. This remains a public-release limitation. No response-time commitment or automated removal process is currently in place.

Young curlers and sensitive information

Public junior competition records may include minors. The project limits its intended scope to curling participation and source provenance, not private contact details, birth dates, home addresses or identity documents. Public availability alone does not settle every privacy or reuse question. Name corrections and identity links require supporting evidence.
