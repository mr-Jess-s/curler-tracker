# Known Limitations

- Archive coverage varies; it is not a complete history of every person.
- Some official source endpoints are unavailable from Vercel egress. A bounded official browser fallback is used and clearly reports partial/incomplete states.
- Separate provincial and national profiles are not merged without defensible identity evidence.
- Physical-device and human assistive-technology testing is outstanding.
- External alert delivery is not configured; Arthur records local task failures.
- A private correction destination and formal retention/dispute process need owner approval.
- Media, mascot, trademark and source-reuse rights are not documented as cleared.
- Controlled staging load passed, but national traffic capacity is not proven.
- OneDrive cloud synchronization to another device is not verified.
- Production rollback automation is implemented; a deliberate live rollback was not performed because it would interrupt the verified release.
