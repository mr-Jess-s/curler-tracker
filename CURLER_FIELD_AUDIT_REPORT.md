## Interpretation and limits

REFERENCE_HISTORY_MATCH means all events in this independently checked reference subset appear; it does not mean a complete career. HISTORY_MATCH_LIVE_SEARCH_INCOMPLETE retains a live-source availability failure. PARTIAL_HISTORY identifies precise missing verified eventIDs in the JSON. VERIFIED_EXTERNAL_HISTORY_NOT_SHOWN identifies an independently documented Brier appearance that the UI did not show. EXTERNAL_BRIER_ROSTER_MATCH requires the independently read officialBrier roster plus a freshI/Oevent roster and exact returned source-nativeID/eventID, not a matching title alone. CROSS_SOURCE_OR_ID_REVIEW needs manual identity/provenance review. Only a confirmed source roster/score conflict should become a wrong-person or wrong-score defect.

Current/upcoming checks apply only to independently observed source fixtures and dated rosters. If the selected sources supply no live/upcoming assignment, the corresponding behaviour remains untested, notpassed. The base/current-two-prior-season collection plus older spotchecks do not encompass all internet records. Unpublished rosters were not fetched. Any source errors and collection limits remain in reference.json.gz. Sources may change between retrieval and UIsearch; live-score differences require a fresh recheck.

This audit changes no production code/data and sends no feedback or other messages. Detailed observations, URLs, retrieval timestamps, payload hashes, missing eventIDs, scores and checkpoints accompany this report.
