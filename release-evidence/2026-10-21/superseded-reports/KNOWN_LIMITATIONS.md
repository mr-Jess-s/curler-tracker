- Production still runs commit `ffa60f3`; candidate-only search, cache and operational repairs are not yet public.
- The production source proxy returns 502. A bounded direct-source browser fallback preserves some functionality but is unsuitable for a national traffic surge.
- Archive coverage varies and is not a complete history of every person.
- Separate source-scoped identities are not merged without defensible evidence.
- Physical-device and human assistive-technology testing are outstanding.
- External alert delivery is not configured; Arthur records failures locally.
- A private correction destination and formal retention/dispute process need owner approval.
- Media, mascot, trademark and source-reuse rights are not documented as cleared.
- Existing controlled load evidence does not prove national capacity.
- OneDrive cloud synchronization to another device is not verified.
- Scheduled tasks use an interactive-user logon and require Arthur to remain powered on and signed in.
- A deliberate live rollback was not performed because it would interrupt the working public deployment.
